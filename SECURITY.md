# Security

Mellora is non-custodial: it never holds keys or funds, and every transaction is built for your wallet and signed
by you. Still, a bug in how a transaction is built or shown could cost a user money, so we want to hear about it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@mellora.org** with "Security" in the subject. Please include:

- what you found and where (page, network, action);
- steps to reproduce it, or a transaction hash;
- what a user could lose because of it.

Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability, and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you share
details. We'll reply as soon as we can.

## Scope

- [app.mellora.org](https://app.mellora.org) and [mellora.org](https://mellora.org)
- The transactions Mellora builds for your wallet on Elysium and Robinhood Chain
- The Telegram alert bots [@mellora_elysium_bot](https://t.me/mellora_elysium_bot) and
  [@mellora_robinhood_bot](https://t.me/mellora_robinhood_bot)

Uniswap's, KyberSwap's and the networks' own contracts are out of scope: report those to their teams.

## Stay safe

Mellora never asks for your seed phrase or private key, never DMs first, and has not launched a token. Official
news only at [mellora.org](https://mellora.org) and [@MelloraLP](https://x.com/MelloraLP).
