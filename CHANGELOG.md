# Changelog

What shipped on [app.mellora.org](https://app.mellora.org), newest first. Dates are UTC.

## 2026-11-25 — Swap with a bridge

- Move funds between networks right inside Swap: from Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, BNB Chain, Polygon,
  Unichain or HyperEVM into Robinhood Chain or Elysium, and back.
- Routes from Relay, LI.FI and Across, side by side: the provider, fees and gas in USD, the time and the minimum
  received are shown before you sign. No Mellora fee on bridges.
- Each transfer is followed until it lands, also after a page reload: sent, filled, received, with both transactions.
- A new token picker: network first, then token, with logos and your balance on every network in USD.

## 2026-11-17 — Robinhood Chain (Beta)

- Robinhood Chain (chain 4663) on app.mellora.org, next to Elysium: a network switch in the header that also
  follows the wallet.
- Pools, Uniswap v3 and v4 positions, swaps (Uniswap or KyberSwap, the better quote).
- Robinhood Stock Tokens in a view of their own, with tickers, company logos and market caps.
- Range alerts for Robinhood Chain: [@mellora_robinhood_bot](https://t.me/mellora_robinhood_bot).
- Elysium's alerts move to [@mellora_elysium_bot](https://t.me/mellora_elysium_bot).
- mellora.org shows both networks' live markets.

## 2026-11-10 — Positions and swaps on Elysium

- Create, collect, move and close Uniswap v4 positions on Elysium.
- Grids: a ladder of ranges placed in one transaction.
- Swaps: the better quote of Uniswap and KyberSwap.

## 2026-11-09 — Elysium public mainnet

- Mellora moves to Elysium's public network data.
- Range alerts on Telegram.

## 2026-11-06 — Elysium soft launch

- Read-only market view on Elysium: pools, prices, charts, trades and liquidity depth.
- Range Runner: a two-minute game on real on-chain prices.
